arc_easy_test_20.jsonl: the first 20 rows of the ARC-Easy test split,
byte-identical to the lines the harness fetcher caches from the HuggingFace
mirror allenai/ai2_arc, config ARC-Easy (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/ai2_arc).

Source: Clark, Cowhey, Etzioni, Khot, Sabharwal, Schoenick, Tafjord. "Think you
have Solved Question Answering? Try ARC, the AI2 Reasoning Challenge." 2018.
https://allenai.org/data/arc
